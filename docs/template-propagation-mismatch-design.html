Template edits reach cards that are already running

Design mock for the card "Handle structural mismatches in template propagation gracefully". Today, if you add or remove a step in a template, every card already part-way through that template is left on the old steps, without telling you. After this change those cards pick up the edit for the work still to come, and the steps already done or being worked on are never touched.

The green-edged steps are locked: done, being worked on, waiting for your approval, failed or skipped steps are never changed, moved or removed. The "Added" and "Removed" labels are only in this mock; on the real card the steps simply look like normal waiting steps.

The rules, in plain words

  • Locked steps never change. A step that is done, being worked on, waiting for approval, failed or skipped keeps its wording, its place and its agent.
  • Steps still waiting follow the template. Their wording, role and settings are updated, as today. A step is recognised by its name; a renamed step is recognised by staying in the same place.
  • New template steps are added to the part of the card still to come, in the template's order.
  • Removed template steps are removed from the part still to come, but only if nobody has written anything on them. A waiting step with a comment or saved work stays, and the save message says so.
  • A new step placed before work that is already done is not added (decision A below).

3. Guide page wording (Workflows guide, "Template Edit Propagation")

Template Edit Propagation

When you edit a template's steps, cards already using it follow the edit for the work they have not reached yet. Steps that are done, being worked on, waiting for approval, failed or skipped are never changed. Waiting steps get the new wording and settings; a step is recognised by its name, or by its place if you renamed it. Steps you add are added to the part of the card still to come, and steps you remove are taken out of it, unless someone has commented on that step or saved work on it. A step you add before work a card has already done is not added to that card. A card that has not started at all is simply switched to the new steps. When a step's role changes, its agent is re-picked for the new role; a step whose role is unchanged keeps its agent, including a hand-picked one.

4. Decisions for Nick

A. A new step placed before work the card has already done. Recommended: don't add it to that card (the card is past that point). Alternative: add it as the next step to run, so it runs before the rest of the remaining work.
B. Removed steps. Recommended: take them off the card if nobody has written on them; keep them if they have comments or saved work. Alternative: never delete, mark them "skipped" so they stay visible.
C. Archived cards. Recommended: leave archived cards alone when a template changes. Alternative: keep updating them, as today.
D. The save message. Recommended: show the short report above. Alternative: keep "Template updated" and report only to agents.
E. Cards already stranded. 45 open, part-way-through cards are on an older version of their template right now. Recommended: don't touch them automatically; this change adds a preview so an agent can show you what a re-sync would do to them first.
