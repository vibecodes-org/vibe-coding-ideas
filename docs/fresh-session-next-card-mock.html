Finish a card, then start the next one fresh

Today one terminal session goes from card to card, so its conversation keeps growing and every action re-reads all of it. Here are three ways for the session to stop at the end of a card and offer you a fresh session for the next one. You can always say no and carry on as today. Anything new has a blue dashed outline; the rest is the terminal panel as it is now.

What all three have in common

The offer comes only when a card's whole workflow has finished (not when a step stops for your approval: the session needs its memory of the card for that). "Next up" is the top card in To Do, then Backlog, the same rule the board's own "Launch Claude Code" uses. The fresh session is the same kind as the one finishing (Claude stays Claude, Codex stays Codex) and starts with the same start-up message a card's "..." menu launch uses today. If the session is still looking after other cards, it says so and stays open for them.

Option A: the session tells you, you start it

Only the session's words change. You start the next card yourself from its "..." menu, which already opens it in a new tab.

The session says the same words as in B, and a strip appears at the foot of its tab with a button. Your click starts the fresh session, so a session can never start one on its own. Typing "keep going here" in the chat still works and hides the strip.

Same tab or new tab?

Shown for option C; the same choice applies to B.

New tab RECOMMENDED
  • The old conversation stays until you close it, so you can still check what it did.
  • Safe when the session is still looking after other cards: nothing it is running gets cut off.
  • The new session runs in its own working copy while the old one is open (that's how two sessions on one board already avoid treading on each other). Once you close the old one, the next fresh session goes back to the main folder.
  • Tabs can pile up: the strip's "Close this session" is there for that.

  • Tidier: one tab per card, nothing to close.
  • The new session works in the main folder, not a separate copy.
  • Ends the old session: can't be offered while it's still looking after other cards, so it would need a fallback to "new tab" then.
  • The finished conversation disappears from view (resume it from My sessions).

I recommend no change: the same start-up message a card's "..." menu launch sends today. Everything the session needs is on the card itself. The board-connection lines are shortened here; they're unchanged.

Your decisions

  1. Which option: A, B or C? I recommend C: one click, and only your click starts a session.
  2. New tab or same tab? I recommend new tab: it never cuts off a session that is still looking after other cards, and you keep the old conversation to look back at.
  3. The wording above: change anything you like. I recommend keeping the fresh session's start-up message as it is today.
  4. Offer only when the whole workflow finishes, not when a step stops for your approval. I recommend this.
  5. Where the new strip differs from existing panel controls (CLAUDE.md asks me to list these):
    • It's a strip along the foot of the tab. The panel has no strip like it today: the nearest are the "This task already has a terminal running" box (a pop-up) and the full-panel "session ended" screen. A pop-up would cover the chat, so I used a strip.
    • Its buttons use the same colours as that box: green for "start something new", plain for "back out". "Close this session" is an outline button, like "Start again" on the ended screen.
    • On a phone it shows no start button, matching the card menu's "Launching runs on your Mac" rule.
Measure after a week: the main window's share of usage against the spike's starting figure (about 23% on the heaviest session).
