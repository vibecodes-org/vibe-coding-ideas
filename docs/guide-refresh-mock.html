Guide /Getting Started

Getting Started

Create your account

Sign up with GitHub, Google, or your email address. Every new account gets 10 free AI credits to try the AI features. After that you add your own Anthropic key in Settings.

The welcome steps

The first time you sign in, five short steps get your first project running:

  1. Welcome: what VibeCodes does.
  2. Profile: your name and a line about you.
  3. Project: type one line about what you want to build. Pick what kind of project it is (Web Application is picked for you), and press Draft with AI if you'd like it written up properly. Drafting uses one AI credit. "Project Kit" step. Onboarding AI is free.
  4. Connect: one Launch Claude Code button connects Claude Code to your new board. No Claude Code yet? Open Manual setup for the command to run.
  5. Done: you land on your board, already filled with cards. Building those first cards is free.

Your dashboard

Until you've finished setting up, a Getting set up checklist sits at the top and ticks itself off as you go. A separate first-run dashboard. Below it are your boards, your tasks, your agents and recent activity. Drag the panels into the order you like; Reset puts them back.

Starting another project

Press New Idea. The form asks for:

  • A title and a line or two. Draft with AI writes it up from one line; once there's text it becomes Expand with AI. Undo brings back what you had.
  • What kind of project is this? Web Application, Mobile App, API / Backend, AI App / Agent, Game, or Custom to start from scratch. A preview shows the team and workflows you'll get.
  • Tags: up to 10.
  • GitHub repo (optional).
  • Who can see it: ideas start public. Switch to private if only you and your collaborators should see it.
Create idea & apply kit sets up the team, workflows and labels and takes you straight to the new board. Press AI Generate Tasks there to fill it with cards.

Finding other people's ideas

The FeedIdeas (in the top bar, once you're signed in) lists public ideas. Switch between All, My Ideas and Collaborating, search, filter by status or tag, and sort by Newest, Most Popular or Most Discussed.

Guide /Launching Claude Code or Codex

Launching Claude Code or Codex

Showing the top of the page only. The rest keeps its detail, with the fixes listed under "Every change".

Overview

Hit Launch and an agent starts work on your board. It connects to VibeCodes, opens the right folder, and offers you the next card. Pick Claude Code or Codex: each runs on your own Mac or Windows PC and uses your own Claude or ChatGPT account.

The button remembers the agent you used last. New accounts start on Claude Code.

Two places it can run

  • In the browser terminal (Beta): a terminal docked under your cards. It needs the small VibeCodes helper app on a Mac or Windows computer; the first time, the terminal walks you through installing it. Run up to 10 at once, side by side if you like, and type into any of them to steer.
  • In a terminal window on your computer, the classic way.

Where you can launch from

  • Board toolbar: the Launch button, plus the arrow beside it for every other way to launch.
  • A card's … menu: Launch in Claude Code, Launch in browser terminal, Launch in Codex, and Launch Codex in browser terminal. Only "Launch in Claude Code".
  • The card window: the terminal icon at the top.
  • The connect banner on a board, the dashboard's Getting set up checklist, and the welcome steps.

Your code and privacy

Your code never leaves your computer.The agent runs on your computer, so your code stays there. When you use the browser terminal, what the terminal shows travels through VibeCodes to reach your browser, and it is not encrypted end to end: the label on each session says so. The folder you pick for a board is saved to your account, so it follows you between browsers.

Guide /Settings

Settings

New page

Open Settings from your picture in the top bar. Everything about your own account lives here:

ProfileYour name, picture, a line about you, and how people can reach you.
NotificationsWhich alerts you get: comments and votes on your ideas, people joining or asking to join, status updates, @mentions, new discussions and replies, and agent alerts. You can also switch email on or off.
Board DefaultsThe columns every new board starts with. Out of the box: Backlog, To Do, Blocked/Requires User Input, In Progress, Verify and Done.
AI API KeyAdd your own Anthropic key and the AI buttons stop using credits.
Model TiersWhich model, and how hard it thinks, for each kind of workflow step, for Claude and for Codex. Also how browser terminal sessions start.
MCP API KeysKeys for connecting tools such as Codex to VibeCodes without the sign-in pop-up.
GitHubConnect GitHub to pick one of your repos, or make a new one, when you link an idea.
Manage agentsOpens your agents.
