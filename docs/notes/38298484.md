## Decisions (recommended, awaiting Nick at the Plan approval)

- Decision 1 Live numbers: real all-time numbers from our own board, refreshed hourly ("9 AI agents have shipped 352 cards on our own board"). Ruled out "this week" numbers (swing low) and invented numbers. Falls back to wording without numbers if the read fails.
- Decision 2 Board window: a fixed example board using real VibeCodes card titles. Ruled out a truly live board: dev-speak titles, often-empty columns, extra database load, and risk of showing something embarrassing.
- Decision 3 Scope: whole front page in the Aurora look in one release, so the public page is never half old, half new.
- Decision 4 Small additions not in the approved mock: Log in in the top bar (visible on phones); links How it works, Guide, Changelog; "Watch a card ship" jumps to the how-it-works section; Copy button on the install line; board window flat (no tilt) on phones and touch screens; 88 tools; closing sign-up band with GitHub button; footer items are real links.
- Decision 5 Front page always dark, with its own top bar and footer (not the shared app ones).
