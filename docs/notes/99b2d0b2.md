## Facts checked (origin/master, live data read-only)
- The run is marked finished by checkAndCompleteRun (src/lib/workflow-helpers.ts) from 4 board-tool paths:
  complete_step, approve_step, skip_step (mcp-server/src/tools/workflows.ts), plus the web page's own
  approve / complete / skip actions (src/actions/workflow.ts).
- Last 14 days on this board: 40 finished runs, 30 of them ended on a step that needed Nick's approval.
  So complete_step alone would miss 3 in 4 finishes. The reply must also go on approve_step and on
  claim_next_step when the card's current run is fully finished.
- claim_next_step today returns {done:true, message:"All steps complete or no pending steps"} both when the
  run is finished AND when the only open step is awaiting approval (pickNextOpenStep skips those). The new
  reply must only fire when every step of the live run is completed or skipped.
- When Nick approves the last step on the web page, nothing reaches the session (no nudge exists).
- "Top of To Do, then Backlog" already exists in code: get_board up_next (mcp-server/src/tools/board-read.ts,
  UP_NEXT_COLUMNS, order by position then id, archived excluded). Reuse it.
- Card menu item is "Launch in browser terminal" (launch-claude-code-button.tsx TASK_ICON_LABEL_BROWSER).
- Session identity (VIBECODES_SESSION) is only auto-filled for the send-message tool by the Mac helper's hook
  (terminal/bridge/src/peer-bridge.js). Making the server tell in-browser sessions apart on complete_step
  would need a helper release, so this card does not try.
- Guide pages to touch: src/app/guide/workflows/page.tsx (the "Loop" step and the Tip), and
  src/app/guide/launching-claude-code/page.tsx (the "Pick up board work" paragraph).
- Existing tests that pin the old reply: mcp-server/src/tools/workflows.test.ts ~826 and ~7756.

## Decisions (proposed, awaiting Nick)
- Split: keep two cards. This one (server reply + next-card choice + Option A wording + guide) ships first and is
  useful on its own. The strip card builds on it. Not one change.
- Next card also skips cards whose workflow already has a step in progress or awaiting approval (someone is on
  it), and the finished card itself. Recommended; Nick approved "top of To Do then Backlog" without this.
- Wording: "is finished." rather than the mock's "is finished and in Verify": at that moment the card may not
  have been moved yet.
- Web-page approval of the last step: no nudge in this card. The session gives the offer the next time it checks
  the card (Nick typing "approved"/"carry on" leads it to call claim_next_step). Candidate follow-up for the
  strip card: show the strip straight from the board when the run finishes.
